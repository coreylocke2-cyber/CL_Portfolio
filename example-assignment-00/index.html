About Myself

My Name is Corey Locke. Although the name alone isn't special, hardly even a rare find, the meaning behind it and what I place it upon myself with are what make it meaningful. I work my hardest, yet know when to stop, I push myself to the limit, and cater to myself on the falling action. I'm an older brother, son, friend, and the creator of my own being, a task that everyone shares but not all try to embody.
