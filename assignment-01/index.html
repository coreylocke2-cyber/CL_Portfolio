My Top 5 Favorite Video Games

  1. Roblox

    Roblox is a popular online platform that allows users to make and create their own games and experiences. It allows creative ideas and imagination to flurish but it has a lot of internal issues. Such as the game being easily manipulated by internet trends, which causes the featured experiences to become more saturated in repetitive content while snuffing out the more thoughtout and creative experiences.

  2. Monkey City

    Monkey City is a once popular mobile game that allowed players to build and manage their own city while defending it from invaders posed as balloons. It's goofy on the surface but in its time it pulled in a lot of people who loved the game and the creators continue to pull in new players with their other games which follow the same premise.

    Although, the game is no longer as popular as it once was due to the lack of attention and care. The other game pulled in more revenue, causing them to abandon it and leave the future of them making a similar game only to the imagination.

  3. Farcry 6

    Far Cry 6 is a first-person shooter game set in a fictional Caribbean island nation. Players take on the role of a guerrilla fighter trying to overthrow a tyrannical dictator who claims that he is making the country a better place by his actions, despite forcing its resident into slave labor, calling them "False Citizens" that don't believe in the vision of their country. The game explores themes of oppression, propaganda, resistance, and the struggle for freedom, providing a narrative that is both engaging and thought-provoking.

  4. Minecraft

    Minecraft is a sandbox game that allows players to build and explore vast, procedurally generated worlds. It encourages creativity and problem-solving, with players able to create everything from simple structures to complex machines. The game has a strong community and a wide range of mods and custom content, making it endlessly replayable.

  5. Assassin's Creed

    Assassin's Creed is a series of action-adventure games that follow the story of assassins throughout history. Players engage in stealth, combat, and exploration, uncovering historical secrets and conspiracies. The series is known for its rich historical settings, complex narratives, and immersive open-world environments which allow for players to truly feel as thought they are involved in the historical events unfolding around them, creating a deep sense of connection to the game world.

These are simply my opionion and personal preferences. Everyone's tastes are different.
